About This Course

Forgiveness is not
weakness. It is freedom.

This is not a course that simply tells you to forgive and move on. It takes you through the honest, sometimes messy reality of what true forgiveness requires — and what it does not. You will learn how to release pain without excusing it, how to protect your identity without hardening your heart, and how to find genuine peace that does not require the other person to change.

Combining Biblical truth with psychological insight and practical tools, this course meets you where you are and walks with you toward where God is calling you to be — free.

  • Biblical foundations from Matthew 6, Philippians 4, and 2 Corinthians 10
  • Practical tools for breaking mental cycles and emotional triggers
  • Honest exploration of boundaries — without guilt or shame
  • A pathway through identity, worth, and healing
  • Suitable for self-study or group use in church or ministry settings

Course Curriculum

6 Modules. 16 Sessions.
One complete pathway to freedom.

Each session includes video teaching, a reflection journal prompt, and a practical exercise. Work through them at your own pace, in your own space.

Module 01
Understanding Forgiveness
What forgiveness is — and what it is not
+

Before you can forgive, you need to understand what you are actually being asked to do — and what you are not. This module dismantles the most common misconceptions about forgiveness and lays a clear Biblical and practical foundation.

  • 01What Forgiveness Is — and What It Is Not Matthew 6:14-15
  • 02Why Forgetting Feels Impossible — and What to Do About It Isaiah 43:25
  • 03The Power of Thoughts — How the Mind Holds On Proverbs 23:7
Module 02
Taking Control of Your Mind
Breaking mental cycles and redirecting thought
+

The mind is a battlefield. This module gives you Biblical and practical tools to interrupt painful thought patterns, choose what you dwell on, and redirect your mental energy toward healing and Kingdom truth.

  • 04The Philippians 4:8 Filter — What You Were Built to Think About Phil 4:8
  • 05Taking Thoughts Captive — The Battle for the Mind 2 Cor 10:5
  • 06Mind Redirection Therapy — Practical Strategies That Work Romans 12:2
Module 03
Applying It in Real Life
When pain keeps returning — triggers, cycles, and the mind-body link
+

Theory must become practice. This module helps you apply everything you have learned in the messy, unpredictable reality of daily life — when the memory returns, when you see the person, when the body responds before the mind catches up.

  • 07When the Pain Keeps Coming Back — Why It Happens and How to Respond Lam 3:22-23
  • 08Handling Emotional Triggers — Responding Instead of Reacting James 1:19
  • 09Mind vs Body — How Trauma Stores Itself and How to Release It Psalm 34:18
Module 04
Boundaries & Self-Protection
Forgiving without handing your peace back over
+

One of the most damaging myths about forgiveness is that it requires you to restore access to the person who hurt you. This module sets the record straight — and equips you to protect yourself with wisdom, not bitterness.

  • 10Forgiveness Does Not Mean Access — Drawing the Line with Clarity Matt 10:16
  • 11Apology vs True Change — How to Tell the Difference Luke 3:8
  • 12Stop Training People to Hurt You — Breaking Patterns Before They Break You Prov 4:23
Module 05
Healing Your Identity
Knowing your worth when pain has distorted your self-image
+

Being hurt by someone can alter the way you see yourself. This module rebuilds the foundation of identity — grounding your worth in who God says you are rather than in what someone did to you or said about you.

  • 13Knowing Your Worth — Identity That Cannot Be Taken Ps 139:14 · Eph 1:4
  • 14When You're Not Ready to Forgive — An Honest Conversation Mark 9:24
Module 06
Living Free
Choosing peace daily — and never going back
+

Freedom is not a destination you arrive at once — it is a life you choose to live daily. This final module equips you with the disciplines, declarations, and perspective you need to sustain genuine peace long after the course is complete.

  • 15Choosing Peace Daily — The Discipline of the Renewed Mind Isaiah 26:3
  • 16Living Without Mental Bondage — Your New Normal John 8:36 · Gal 5:1
