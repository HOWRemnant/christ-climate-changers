Course Curriculum

10 Modules of Kingdom Discipleship

Each module builds toward a single goal — raising believers who can faithfully disciple others, generation after generation.

Module 01
The Foundation of Discipleship — Jesus' Model

Understand the Biblical pattern of discipleship Jesus established — investing deeply in a few to reach the many.

  • Why Jesus discipled twelve, not multitudes
  • The shape of a discipling relationship
  • Discipleship vs. mere instruction
Matthew 28:19-20
Module 02
Personal Growth — Becoming Before Leading

A disciple-maker must first be a growing disciple. Establish the personal habits of spiritual formation.

  • Daily rhythms of prayer and the Word
  • Self-reflection and personal accountability
  • Consecration as a lifestyle
Romans 12:1-2
Module 03
Character & Integrity in Discipleship

Disciples are caught as much as they are taught. Build the character that gives your discipleship credibility.

  • Character vs reputation
  • Modelling integrity under pressure
  • Identifying personal blind spots
1 Samuel 16:7
Module 04
Identifying & Selecting Disciples

Learn how to recognise teachable hearts and discern who is ready to be invested in for the long term.

  • Qualities of a teachable spirit
  • Praying before you choose
  • Starting small and starting well
Luke 6:12-13
Module 05
Building Relational Discipleship

Discipleship happens in relationship, not just curriculum. Develop the relational skills that sustain transformation.

  • Creating safe, honest relationships
  • Listening as a discipleship tool
  • Walking alongside, not just ahead
1 Thessalonians 2:8
Module 06
Teaching the Word with Clarity

Equip yourself with simple, faithful tools for opening Scripture in a way that disciples can understand and apply.

  • Simple hermeneutics for everyday disciplers
  • Making the Word practical and applicable
  • Teaching to transform, not just to inform
2 Timothy 2:15
Module 07
Accountability & Spiritual Growth Tracking

Discipleship without accountability rarely produces lasting fruit. Build healthy, grace-filled accountability structures.

  • Asking honest, healthy questions
  • Tracking growth without legalism
  • Correction wrapped in grace
Proverbs 27:17
Module 08
Navigating Challenges in Discipleship

Discipleship is rarely smooth. Learn to navigate resistance, setbacks, and seasons of slow growth with wisdom.

  • Handling resistance and discouragement
  • When a disciple stalls or drifts
  • Persevering through the hard seasons
Galatians 6:9
Module 09
Multiplication — Disciples Who Disciple

The true measure of discipleship is multiplication. Equip your disciples to become disciple-makers themselves.

  • The multiplication mandate
  • Releasing disciples to lead others
  • Building a multi-generational pattern
2 Timothy 2:2
Module 10 — Capstone
Equipping Kingdom Carriers for the Nations

The final module casts vision beyond the local — equipping disciples with a heart and strategy for global Kingdom impact.

  • From local discipleship to global carrying
  • Building your personal discipleship plan
  • Completing the certificate and next steps
Acts 1:8

No prior ministry experience is required. This course is designed for any believer with a heart to disciple others well — whether you are leading a small group already or simply sensing the call to invest in someone else's growth.
