Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Do I need to be a Christian to receive coaching?+
Our coaching is unapologetically Biblical and Kingdom-centred. It is designed for believers who want to grow in their faith, calling, and leadership. While we warmly welcome anyone, the content is rooted in Scripture and will be most transformational for those who share that foundation.
How do I know which coaching pathway is right for me?+
That is exactly what the free Discovery Call is for. In 30 minutes, we will discuss where you are, what you are working toward, and what level of support will be most effective. There is no obligation and no pressure — just clarity.
Are sessions held online or in person?+
All 1-to-1 and group coaching sessions are held online via Zoom, making them accessible regardless of your location. Coaching retreat experiences are held in-person at carefully selected luxury venues across the UK.
What is the difference between coaching and counselling?+
Coaching is forward-focused — it works from where you are toward where you want to go. It is action-oriented, goal-directed, and future-facing. Counselling and therapy focus on processing and healing the past. Our coaching incorporates prayer and Biblical truth, but is not a substitute for professional therapeutic support where that is needed.
Can I get coaching through the Suddenly Membership?+
Yes. Suddenly Members on Patreon receive access to monthly group coaching sessions, exclusive coaching content, and priority booking for retreats and intensives. It is the most affordable way to access consistent Kingdom coaching support.
How long does a coaching engagement typically last?+
This varies by pathway. Individual sessions can be booked on a standalone basis. Package programmes typically run 8–12 weeks. Group cohorts run in 3-month cycles. Membership is ongoing. The Discovery Call will help identify what timeframe is most appropriate for your specific goals.
