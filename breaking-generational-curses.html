Course Overview

What is a generational curse?

A generational curse is a spiritual bondage — a demonic access point, a divine consequence of unrepented sin, or an inherited covenantal tie — that follows a family line until it is deliberately and specifically broken through the authority of Christ and the application of His blood.

Deuteronomy 28 is the most complete description of curses in Scripture — listing with extraordinary specificity the consequences of covenant disobedience: mental breakdown, recurring sickness, persistent poverty, family collapse, premature death, failure and fruitlessness. Many people reading that list recognise their family history on its pages. This course gives those recognitions a Biblical name and a Biblical solution.

The solution is not deliverance as performance, not spiritual warfare as entertainment, and not the repetition of formulas. The solution is the finished work of Jesus Christ — applied with understanding, faith, and Biblical authority through repentance, renunciation, and declaration.

Recognise the Bondage

Seven signs of a generational curse

Derek Prince identified seven key Biblical indicators of a generational curse operating in a family line. These are grounded in Deuteronomy 28 — not superstition. They are spiritual markers with real causes, and real solutions.

🧠
Mental & Emotional Breakdown

Recurring depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, or emotional collapse appearing across multiple generations with no clear medical cause. The same breakdown, at similar ages, through similar triggers — generation after generation.

Deuteronomy 28:28 · 2 Timothy 1:7
🤒
Repeated or Hereditary Sickness

Specific diseases appearing consistently in a family line — cancer at the same age, the same chronic conditions — beyond what genetics alone explains. Patterns of sickness that no amount of medical treatment permanently resolves.

Deuteronomy 28:22 · Isaiah 53:5
💸
Persistent Financial Failure

Never enough — money disappears without explanation, businesses fail despite effort, financial advance is always followed by sudden reversal. An invisible hand keeping the family at the bottom regardless of ability or hard work.

Deuteronomy 28:17-18 · Haggai 1:6
💔
Marriage & Family Breakdown

Divorce, infidelity, estrangement, and relational destruction repeating across generations. Family structures that consistently collapse under invisible pressure — something beyond the individuals involved, something systemic.

Deuteronomy 28:41 · Malachi 4:6
☠️
Premature Death

Family members dying young — accidents, sudden illness, violence — with unusual frequency across the bloodline. A spirit of premature death that follows the family line and claims lives before their time in every generation.

Deuteronomy 28:45 · John 10:10
🔄
Chronic Fruitlessness & Failure

Working hard without lasting result. Businesses that never grow. Ministry that never bears fruit. Opportunities that consistently evaporate. The sense of moving forward but never arriving — every generation in the same place as the last.

Deuteronomy 28:38-40 · Haggai 1:9
Biblical Evidence

Generational curses in Scripture

The Bible records multiple accounts of curses entering family lines — their cause, their trajectory, and most importantly: the God who has the power to break every one of them through the cross of His Son.

🏔
Noah's Son
Ham & Canaan — The Curse of Dishonour
Genesis 9:25-27
"Cursed be Canaan! The lowest of slaves will he be to his brothers."

Noah's curse on Canaan — following Ham's deliberate act of dishonour and exposure — produced an entire people group under spiritual bondage. The Canaanites' subsequent idolatry, child sacrifice, and sexual depravity demonstrates how a spoken curse, when received and lived into, opens a bloodline to spiritual occupation across many generations.

Entry point: Dishonour of a parent — spoken curse received as identity
⚔️
King David
David's House — The Sword That Never Left
2 Samuel 12:10-12
"Now therefore, the sword shall never depart from your house, because you despised me and took the wife of Uriah the Hittite to be your own wife."

Nathan's prophetic pronouncement over David was not a private prediction — it was a divinely-announced curse consequent on covenant violation. David sinned in secret; the consequences were public and generational. Amnon raped Tamar. Absalom murdered Amnon. Absalom led a rebellion. Adonijah attempted to seize the throne. The sword remained.

Entry point: Sexual sin and murder — prophetic curse on a royal bloodline
💛
Elisha's Servant
Gehazi — Greed and Leprosy
2 Kings 5:27
"Naaman's leprosy will cling to you and to your descendants forever."

Elisha pronounced a specific, named, generational curse upon Gehazi and his offspring for lying and greed. The curse was precise — a named disease, a named family line, a named timeframe: forever. This is one of the clearest Biblical examples of a curse explicitly extended to descendants because of an ancestor's sin.

Entry point: Greed, deception and dishonour of prophetic ministry
🏺
Achan's Household
Achan — Sin Under the Ban
Joshua 7:24-26
"Achan son of Zerah... his sons and daughters... were brought to the Valley of Achor."

Achan took what was under God's ban — devoted things from Jericho — and hid them in his tent. Israel's army was defeated as a result. His entire household bore the consequences. This powerful account illustrates how one person's act of covenant violation brings a curse on the household, not just the individual — and how that curse must be specifically identified and removed.

Entry point: Hidden sin, stealing what belongs to God, corporate consequence
🩸
The Jerusalem Crowd
A Self-Imposed Blood Curse
Matthew 27:25
"His blood be on us and on our children!"

The crowd's declaration at Christ's crucifixion is a sober example of a corporate, self-imposed spoken curse. Words of blood guilt spoken over a generation carry real spiritual weight — they are a covenant, not merely an emotion. Many people unknowingly carry the weight of self-imposed word curses spoken over their own families by angry, wounded, or ignorant ancestors.

Entry point: Self-imposed spoken curse — words of blood and guilt
🌿
The Rechabites
Jonadab — A Generational Blessing
Jeremiah 35:18-19
"Jonadab son of Rekab will never fail to have a descendant to serve me."

God's promise to the Rechabites is the positive proof of the principle: what travels as a curse can also travel as a blessing. Because they obeyed their forefather's commands across generations, God established an eternal blessing on their line. The breaking of a curse is not the end goal — the receiving of the blessing is. You are not just being freed from something. You are being freed into something.

Proof: Generational obedience produces generational blessing — the goal
How It Looks Today

Generational curses in the modern world

The Deuteronomy 28 curse list reads like a diagnosis of many family systems today. These are not coincidences — they are spiritual patterns with identifiable causes that require spiritual solutions. The cross has already provided those solutions.

🏥
Cancer & Hereditary Disease

The same cancer appearing in the same location, at similar ages, across three or four generations of the same family — beyond what genetics alone can account for. A spirit of infirmity operating through a family bloodline until specifically and spiritually addressed.

Deuteronomy 28:22, 35 · Isaiah 53:5
🔁
Cyclical Poverty Resisting Every Effort

Money that arrives and disappears. Businesses launched and closed. Inheritances lost. Every financial advance followed by sudden reversal — as if an unseen force consistently strips what the family builds. Hard work produces nothing lasting.

Deuteronomy 28:17-18 · Haggai 1:6
😵
Suicide & Premature Death Patterns

Multiple family members taking their own lives, or dying young through violence, accident, or unexplained illness. A spirit of premature death identifiable across generations — the same end coming to people in the same family at the same stage of life.

John 10:10 · Deuteronomy 28:45
🔮
Freemasonry & Secret Society Legacy

A grandfather's membership in Freemasonry or other secret societies brings oaths taken in darkness into the family bloodline. These oaths — sworn on penalty of physical harm, often invoking false gods — create legal access points for demonic activity in every subsequent generation until specifically renounced and cancelled.

Exodus 20:3-5 · Deuteronomy 18:10-12
🧿
Witchcraft, Ancestral Worship & Occult

Grandparents' or great-grandparents' involvement in traditional witchcraft, ancestral veneration, voodoo, Santeria, or new-age spirituality create spiritual covenants that remain active across generations — producing oppression, spiritual blindness, and resistance to the Gospel in their descendants until broken.

Deuteronomy 18:9-12 · Acts 19:18-19
😶
Word Curses Spoken Over the Family

"You will never amount to anything." "The men in this family always fail." "We never get ahead." Careless, bitter, or deliberately malicious words spoken by parents, grandparents, or authority figures function as self-fulfilling spiritual declarations — curses in the truest sense — that shape identity and destiny across generations.

Proverbs 18:21 · Matthew 12:36-37
🤝
Ungodly Soul Ties & Blood Oaths

Sexual covenants, blood brotherhoods, initiation oaths, gang initiations, and binding agreements entered by ancestors create spiritual ties that remain active. These covenants grant legal access to demonic forces that follow the bloodline until every soul tie is specifically identified and severed through the blood of Christ.

1 Corinthians 6:16 · Numbers 30:2
🚧
Blocked Destiny & Persistent Frustration

Everything attempted is blocked. Doors open and immediately close. Opportunities evaporate. Qualifications lead nowhere. Relationships promised don't materialise. The sense — confirmed generation after generation — that something unseen is working systematically against the family's ability to fulfil its potential.

Deuteronomy 28:29 · Isaiah 43:18-19
👻
Demonic Visitation & Spiritual Harassment

Nightmares, sleep paralysis, and demonic presence that follow family members identically across generations — the same experiences described by grandmother, mother, and daughter; by grandfather, father, and son. Spiritual harassment that is not individual but familial — attached to the bloodline, not just the person.

Psalm 91:5-6 · Ephesians 6:12

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Doesn't salvation automatically break all curses?+
Galatians 3:13 declares that Christ has legally redeemed us from the curse — past tense, accomplished fact. But as with all aspects of salvation, what is legally ours must be practically appropriated by faith. Just as we are legally healed by His stripes yet must receive healing in practice, we must actively enforce our freedom from generational curses. This course teaches you how to do exactly that — applying what is already legally yours through repentance, declaration, and faith.
Is it dangerous to pray deliverance prayers?+
A believer standing in the authority of the name of Jesus, covered by the blood of Christ, and praying in accordance with Scripture is not in danger — they are exercising the authority Christ gave every believer in Luke 10:19. This course approaches deliverance from a position of victory, not fear. You are not going into battle to fight for victory — you are enforcing a victory already won at Calvary.
What if I don't know about occult involvement in my family?+
Many people don't have full knowledge of their family history. The course addresses this specifically — you pray over what you know, ask the Holy Spirit to reveal what you don't, and make comprehensive declarations covering every occult involvement, named and unnamed, known and unknown. God honours faith-filled prayer even when your information is incomplete. He knows your full family line even when you don't.
Can a born-again Christian still be under a generational curse?+
Yes — in the sense that the legal grounds for a curse may not have been specifically addressed even after salvation. A person can be genuinely born again and yet still experience the effects of generational bondage because the doors opened by their ancestors have not been identified and deliberately closed. This is not a reflection on their salvation — it is an invitation to a deeper dimension of freedom that is already available to them in Christ.
Should I take this with the Generational Sin course?+
We strongly recommend taking both. The Generational Sin course addresses the repeated behavioural patterns — the human dimension. This course addresses the spiritual bondages behind those patterns — the demonic dimension. Together they provide the most complete Biblical approach to generational freedom.
