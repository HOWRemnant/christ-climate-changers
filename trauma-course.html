Course Overview

Healing that begins
with being understood

Trauma can leave wounds that affect the heart, mind, body, relationships, and spiritual life. This course helps you understand trauma through biblical wisdom, prayer, reflection, and practical healing principles rooted in Christ.

You will not walk this alone. Each module gently guides you from understanding to processing to restoration — at a pace that honours where you are, with Scripture and prayer as your anchor throughout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this a replacement for professional therapy?+
No. This course offers biblical teaching, prayer, and reflection to support your healing journey, but it is not a substitute for professional therapeutic or medical support where that is needed. If you are in crisis or experiencing severe trauma symptoms, please also seek qualified professional care.
How long do I have access to the course?+
You receive lifetime access to all 8 modules, the workbook, and prayer resources. Work through the material at whatever pace feels right for you, and revisit it whenever you need to.
Will I need to share my story publicly?+
No. This is a self-paced, private course. All journaling and reflection exercises are for your own use, and nothing is shared unless you choose to.
Do I need previous Bible knowledge to take this course?+
Not at all. Every module clearly explains the Scripture it draws from, making this course accessible whether you are new to faith or have walked with Christ for years.
Is there a certificate at the end?+
Yes. On completing all 8 modules, you will receive a Certificate of Completion from Christ's Climate Changers, which many use for personal milestone, ministry, or pastoral care service records.
