What Is The CCC Community

A digital home for
believers walking together

The CCC Community is a free, members-first online home — a space where believers from across the world gather to grow, encourage one another, and walk out their faith together, not alone.

Whether you are working through a CCC course, seeking daily encouragement, in need of prayer, or simply looking for Kingdom-minded fellowship, this community was built for you. It is the connective thread between everything Christ's Climate Changers offers — courses, coaching, prayer, and the Suddenly membership — all in one place.

  • Completely free to join — no barrier to belonging
  • Active daily discussion, encouragement, and accountability
  • Direct access to CCC courses and learning pathways
  • A safe, moderated space rooted in Biblical truth
  • Live events, Q&As, and prayer gatherings throughout the year

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is the CCC Community really free?+
Yes. The CCC Community on our Dashboard is completely free to join, forever. There is no hidden cost or trial period. Suddenly is our optional premium membership for those who want to go deeper, but it is never required to be part of the core community.
What is Systeme.io, and do I need an account?+
Systeme.io is the platform that hosts our community — it combines discussion, courses, and events in one place. You will need a free Skool account to join, which takes less than a minute to set up.
Do I need to take a course to join the community?+
Not at all. The community is open to anyone — students, course alumni, and those simply looking for Kingdom fellowship. Courses live inside the platform, but joining the community itself has no prerequisite.
What is the difference between the Community and Suddenly?+
The free CCC Community gives you full access to discussion, prayer support, and core resources. Suddenly, our Patreon membership, adds exclusive monthly teaching, prophetic insight, and priority access to retreats and live events on top of that.
Is the community moderated and safe?+
Yes. New members are reviewed before approval, and the community is actively moderated by the CCC team to keep it a safe, genuine, and Biblically grounded space.
Can I cancel my CCC membership anytime?+
Yes, CCC-Membership is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. Your free Kingdom Community access remains regardless of your membership status.
